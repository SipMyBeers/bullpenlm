📢 PUBLISH A POST

Posted on Twitter, LinkedIn, Discord, your blog? Drop the URL + pick where the tracked link should route. We'll mint your token + give you the URL to paste back into the post.

How it works:
1. You write a post pointing at https://app.bullpenlm.com/m/<token>
2. Someone clicks → server logs the click → 302s them to the dest URL with ?ref=<token>
3. If they sign up, the landing page picks up ref → attribution wires back to you → you earn money-XP
4. Same for deals that trace to that lead — money-XP credited to whoever drew them in
