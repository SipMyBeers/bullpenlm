What you become

Six ranks. One ladder.

Stack phase badges → climb the ladder. Every rank is permanent on your rep profile and visible on the team leaderboard.

  1. ROOKIE 0 phases Just signed up. No proof yet.
  2. WALK-ON 1–2 phases Past the front door. Earning the right to be here.
  3. STARTER 3–4 phases You can carry a real call. Quota-eligible.
  4. CLOSER 5–6 phases You handle senior buyers and survive objections.
  5. ALL-STAR All 7 phases Every phase passed. Full Gauntlet survivor.
  6. LEGEND All 7 + 10 real signed deals Public Hall-of-Fame card. Permanent name on the founders page.

Multiplayer

One bullpen.
Your whole team in it.

Run it solo on your laptop, or host it for the team like a co-op dungeon. Everyone shares the CRM, claims their own prospects, and shows up on a live leaderboard.

①

One host

You run BullpenLM on your Mac Mini (or a $10 VPS). One docker compose up. The container handles everything: the AI buyers, the recording, the metrics, the leaderboard.

②

Invite the team

Tailscale-invite your friends. They open the floor in their browser, type their rep name, and they're in. No installs, no accounts, no SaaS bill. Just trusted-network access.

③

Climb together

Each rep claims their own prospects ("claimed by Brad" locks the account for 14 days). All calls log to one CRM. Leaderboard ranks everyone by badges + total calls + signed deals.

⚔

Territory claims

One rep, one prospect at a time. Auto-releases after 14 days if no activity — no squatting on the whole list.

📡

Live activity feed

"Brad just passed Phase II on Allstate." "Mike booked a meeting at Cigna." Real-time scroll so the team feels each other working.

🏆

Shared leaderboard

Every rep's badges, rank, and call count visible to the whole team. Friendly pressure beats Slack reminders.

🔒

Your data, your machine

The CRM file lives on your host. Teammates connect to it; they don't get their own copy. One source of truth, you control the door.

For founders

Got your own product?
Launch your own bullpen.

BullpenLM isn't just KillSesh. Any founder can spin up their own bullpen with their own product, their own commission structure, and their own friends doing the calling. You pick the access mode.

🌍

Public

Anyone can join. No invite code, no application. Great for "come learn to sell with us" communities.

✉

Invite-only

Applications go through a review queue. You generate invite codes one at a time. KillSesh runs this mode.

💳

Paid

Membership fee gates entry. Stripe integration. Set your price; reps pay to access the bullpen + earn commission on their own deals.

Clone the repo · run python3 server/server.py · your bullpen is live on localhost:7878
