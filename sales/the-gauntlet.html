THE GAUNTLET · BullpenLM Incentive Map

What you're playing for. What you earn for passing.

Seven phases. Each one is a real moment a real sales call dies. Survive

the practice version in BullpenLM and you survive the real one.

How the rewards work

Every phase you pass earns you two things:

  • A badge — permanent. Stamped to your rep profile. Shows up next

to your name in BullpenLM, in your team's leaderboard, and on your

public Hall-of-Fame card.

  • A field unlock — the real-world thing you can now do that you

couldn't before. The badge is the receipt; the field unlock is the

reason you bothered.

If you're on Beers Labs' commissioned KillSesh team (i.e. you signed

the Referral Agreement), every phase also tracks to a cash bounty

when you deploy that skill in the field. Bounties stack — they don't

replace your normal commission, they're paid on top of it during your

first 90 days.

THE PHASES

Phase I — Through the gate

The skill: Get past the gatekeeper without burning the lead.

Badge: THE GATEKEEPER'S NOD

A small gold checkmark with a phone-receiver silhouette. Awarded the

first time you pass the Phase I drill (transfer earned in ≤15 sec, no

jargon, no hedges).

Field unlock: You're now cleared to dial House Accounts'

gatekeeper switchboards for research only (still no pitching to the

buyer — that's House territory). For Open Accounts, you can start

warm-dialing immediately.

Cash bounty (internal team): $25 per gatekeeper-transferred

buyer during your first 30 days, capped at $250. Proof: BullpenLM

recording of the gatekeeper transfer + the subsequent buyer conversation

logged under your REP name.

Phase II — Leave a mark

The skill: Voicemail that gets returned. The 30-second one with the

specific hook + the repeated callback number.

Badge: VOICE MEMO

A vintage cassette icon. Awarded when you pass the Phase II drill with a

named-fact hook and a clear callback ask.

Field unlock: Voicemail templates in BullpenLM unlock — pre-built

scripts customized per industry (insurance, banking, healthcare gov).

Plus a "VM-returned" tag in the dashboard so you can track callback

rate by template.

Cash bounty: $50 per voicemail that gets returned in your first

30 days, capped at $500. Proof: outbound recording timestamp + inbound

callback timestamp + transcript.

Phase III — Earn the room

The skill: 60-second pitch to the actual buyer. Failure mode named,

incumbent named, 15-minute briefing asked for.

Badge: THE FLOOR

A microphone with a single stripe. The first one that actually means

something to senior reps — passing Phase III is the line between

"someone teaching themselves to sell" and "an actual rep."

Field unlock: Permission to dial Tier-2 Open Accounts directly.

(Tier 1 — the top 5 highest-value Open prospects — opens at Phase IV.)

You also get an automated reminder to record Phase III pitches for

self-review, which feeds the metrics chart.

Cash bounty: $200 per 15-minute briefing booked in your first

60 days, capped at $2,000. Proof: calendar invite from buyer's email

domain + recording of the booking conversation.

Phase IV — Hold the line

The skill: Defending the pilot price against an incumbent

comparison. No apologies. No hedges. Reframe to value.

Badge: THE LINE

A red vertical bar with a green checkmark. Earned the first time you

pass Phase IV with zero apologetic phrases and a successful reframe

keyword ("audit", "risk", "verification", "compliance").

Field unlock: Tier-1 Open Accounts (top 5) unlock for dialing.

You also get access to the Company's price-objection one-pager and the

authority to send it to a prospect mid-cycle (subject to Dylan's

review of the email draft).

Cash bounty: $500 per signed pilot where the customer cited a

competitor by name in the discovery call (proves they were a tough

buyer, not a layup). Stacks with normal 25% commission.

Phase V — Win the corner office

The skill: 30-second elevator pitch to a CIO/CTO using business risk

language, not tech jargon.

Badge: THE CORNER OFFICE

A simple suit-and-tie silhouette. The badge that signals you can move

upmarket. Reps who never earn this stay stuck at director-level all

their career.

Field unlock: Senior buyer dialing rights. Authority to cold-call

named C-suite contacts at any Open Account. Senior buyers have higher

no-show rates but 4x deal value when they engage.

Cash bounty: $1,000 per signed pilot where the primary buyer was

a CIO, CTO, or EVP-level executive (verified via LinkedIn title at

time of contract). Stacks with normal commission.

Phase VI — The clarity test

The skill: Explain BullpenLM/KillSesh to a non-technical person in

30 seconds using an analogy. No banned words.

Badge: THE CIVILIAN

A small lightning bolt next to a question mark. Looks playful, but it's

the badge senior partners actually respect — clarity is harder than

fluency.

Field unlock: Permission to represent the Company at public

events (Slack groups, founder Discords, conferences) on the Company's

behalf. Before Phase VI, all public outreach goes through Dylan.

Cash bounty: $300 per signed pilot where the deal originated from

a public/community channel (not a cold call) — Slack DM, Indie Hackers

thread, Discord intro, etc. Stacks with normal commission.

Phase VII — Pass the torch

The skill: 60-second post-call recap to your teammate. Structured.

Specific. Names the next step.

Badge: THE BATON

Two interlocking rings. The badge that lets you stop being a lone

operator and start being a teammate. Earned the first time you record a

real post-call recap that follows the Phase VII format.

Field unlock: Mentor status. You can now sit in on another

rep's first 5 calls (with their consent) and your feedback is logged

to their training profile. Beers Labs internal: you become eligible

to take on a second rep underneath you at a 5% sub-commission

on their first-year signed pilots.

Cash bounty: $1,000 mentor bonus the first time a rep you

mentored signs their own first pilot. Stacks with your 5% sub-commission

on that deal.

How to claim a bounty

  • The action triggers automatically in BullpenLM — every time you

pass a phase drill or log a real call under your REP name, the

system records it.

  • At the end of each calendar month, the team rolls up the bounties

you earned that month and adds them to your commission check (per § 4

of the Referral Agreement, paid by the 15th of the following month).

  • Disputes: if you think a call should have triggered a bounty and

didn't (e.g. customer cited a competitor but didn't say the name

on-recording), email Dylan with the call ID and your evidence. He

resolves in good faith based on the recording — decision is final

per § 3 of the Agreement.

Why this exists

Cold calling without a goal is mental torture. Every "no" feels like

proof you suck. Every connect feels like a lottery ticket.

The Gauntlet replaces "did I close?" with "did I make progress?" at

the level of individual moves. Pass the gatekeeper drill → you got

better at the gatekeeper. Pass Phase III → you got better at the open.

Pass them all → you're a complete rep.

Badges are permanent. Field unlocks are progressive. Bounties make

the early grind survivable while you're still learning. By the time you

hit your first $3,750 commission, you've already earned a few hundred

in bounties along the way and you've stopped feeling like you're

working for free.

Show up. Drill. Dial. Get paid for every step.
