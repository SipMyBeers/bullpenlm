## 1. What the Rep does

The Rep identifies, contacts, qualifies, and pitches prospective customers
for KillSesh — primarily through cold calls, voicemails, and email — and
will hand qualified opportunities to the Company for closing, OR close them
directly with the Company's standard pilot terms.

The Rep operates as an independent contractor, sets their own hours, and
uses their own tools. The Rep is **not** an employee, agent, or partner of
the Company and has **no authority** to: sign contracts on the Company's
behalf; make pricing concessions outside the Company's published pricing;
make product or delivery promises outside the Company's published scope;
represent themselves as an employee or officer of the Company.

---

## 2. Commission structure

The Rep earns commission on **collected revenue** (cash actually received by
the Company) from accounts the Rep originates:

| Revenue type                                  | Commission rate |
|-----------------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Pilot revenue (first signed agreement)        | **25 %**        |
| Expansion revenue (additional modules / scope) | **15 %**        |
| Renewal revenue (year 2 and beyond)           | **10 %**        |

**Commission window per account: 24 months** from the date of the first
signed pilot. After 24 months, the account is considered a Company account
and no further commission accrues to the Rep.

---

## 3. What counts as "originating" an account

An account is originated by the Rep if and only if **all** of the following
are true at the time the prospect first engages with the Company:

1. **First contact.** The Rep made the first business contact with a named
   person at that prospect organization. The Rep must be able to prove
   first contact via the Company's CRM timestamp, call recording, or email
   thread.
2. **Active engagement.** The Rep stayed engaged with the prospect up
   until the prospect booked a meeting or expressed buying interest.
3. **No prior Company relationship.** The account was not in the Company's
   CRM as an active opportunity, current customer, former customer (within
   12 months), or named target the Company had already pitched.

If two reps both claim the same account, the Company resolves it in good
faith based on the CRM record; the Company's decision is final.

The Company will provide a list of named, off-limits accounts ("House
Accounts") to the Rep before the Rep begins outreach. The Company will
refresh this list in writing whenever it changes.

---

## 4. Payment terms

Commissions are paid by the Company to the Rep once per month, by the 15th
of the month following collection, via bank transfer or check.

---

## 5. Claw-back

If a customer refunds, charges back, or cancels within the first 90 days
of paying, the corresponding commission is clawed back from the Rep's
next payment. Refunds caused by Company delivery failure do not trigger
a claw-back.

---

## 6. Term and termination

This agreement is at-will and can be terminated by either party with
**30 days' written notice** (email is sufficient). On termination:

- Commissions on accounts in active sales cycles continue to accrue to the
  Rep for **120 days** past termination.
- Commissions on closed customers continue for the full remaining
  **24-month window** after termination.
- The Rep returns or destroys any Company-confidential materials.

---

## 7. Confidentiality

The Rep agrees not to disclose to any third party without the Company's
written consent: customer information, pricing beyond the Company's
publicly stated pricing, product roadmap, or any non-public technical
detail of **KillSesh** or **BullpenLM**.

This obligation survives termination indefinitely for customer
information, and for 3 years for pricing and roadmap.

---

## 8. No exclusivity, no non-compete

The Rep is free to take other contract work, provided that work does not
involve directly competing products (other COBOL-to-modern-stack
translation tools). The Company reserves the same freedom.

---

## 9. Independent-contractor classification

The Rep is an independent contractor, not an employee. The Company will
not withhold income tax, Social Security, Medicare, or workers'
compensation. The Rep pays their own taxes and insurance.

---

## 10. Limitation of liability

Neither party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental,
consequential, or punitive damages. The Company's total liability under
this agreement is capped at the total commissions paid to the Rep in the
12 months preceding the claim.

---

## 11. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon, USA. Any
disputes shall be resolved in the state or federal courts located in
Multnomah County, Oregon, and both parties consent to that venue.

---

## 12. Entire agreement

This document is the entire agreement between the parties on this
subject, and supersedes any prior verbal or written understanding. Any
amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.
