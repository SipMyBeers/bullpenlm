CALL FLOW


1. Dial (stand up · breathe · smile)
2. Gatekeeper → ask for the name + title (don't apologize)
3. Buyer picks up → 30-sec opener (above)
4. THEY ASK QUESTIONS → use the 3-version pitch:
     · Gatekeeper version (no jargon)
     · Buyer version (REDEFINES, field-level parity)
     · Exec version (audit risk, compliance)
5. Ask for the meeting: "Thursday at 2 or Friday at 10?"
6. Book the calendar invite. Get their email.
7. LOG IT in BullpenLM (status = Briefing Booked, REP = your name)

HANDOFFS TO DYLAN (don't try to solve these yourself)

  • Yes to a meeting → invite him to the calendar
  • Hard technical question → "Our CTO will walk you through it Thursday"
  • Pricing past $15K → "Our CEO will walk you through expansion"
  • Signed contract / redlines → immediate handoff
