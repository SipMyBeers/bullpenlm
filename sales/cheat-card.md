# THE BULLPEN CHEAT CARD · pin this

---

## OPENER (memorize)

> "Hi [first name] — Dylan from Beers Labs. I'll be quick.
> Your team is probably evaluating COBOL modernization tools right now
> with the major SIs. There's a quiet field-drop problem in those tools
> that shows up as audit findings 6 months later. We solve it.
>
> **Worth 15 minutes Thursday to show your team how it works?**"

Then SHUT UP. Count to 3.

---

## GATEKEEPER (15 sec)

> "Hi, this is [your name] — could you connect me to [exact name],
> the [exact title]?"

If asked what for: *"COBOL modernization roadmap — audit-verification
layer. She's expecting a follow-up."*

NEVER use the words: COBOL, REDEFINES, cryptographic, field-level parity.

---

## THE 5 OBJECTIONS · 5 RESPONSES

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We have a solution" | "We're not a replacement — we're the verification layer underneath. 15 min?" |
| "Send materials" | "Happy to. Which SI are you working with so I send the right one?" |
| "No budget" | "Comes out of existing modernization line, not new. Worth a 15-min look?" |
| "Why are you calling me?" | "Because [specific fact about them]. 30 seconds and you decide." |
| "Not my role" | "Got it — who owns COBOL verification work? I'll save us both a step." |

## CALL FLOW

```
1. Dial (stand up · breathe · smile)
2. Gatekeeper → ask for the name + title (don't apologize)
3. Buyer picks up → 30-sec opener (above)
4. THEY ASK QUESTIONS → use the 3-version pitch:
     · Gatekeeper version (no jargon)
     · Buyer version (REDEFINES, field-level parity)
     · Exec version (audit risk, compliance)
5. Ask for the meeting: "Thursday at 2 or Friday at 10?"
6. Book the calendar invite. Get their email.
7. LOG IT in BullpenLM (status = Briefing Booked, REP = your name)
```

## HANDOFFS TO DYLAN (don't try to solve these yourself)

- Yes to a meeting → invite him to the calendar
- Hard technical question → "Our CTO will walk you through it Thursday"
- Pricing past $15K → "Our CEO will walk you through expansion"
- Signed contract / redlines → immediate handoff

- Don't trash competitors. ("Accenture's great at X, we handle Y.")
