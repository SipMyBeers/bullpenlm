## 1. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by
one Party to the other, marked or reasonably identifiable as
confidential, including: customer data, pricing, product roadmap,
financial information, source code, sales scripts, training materials,
and internal communications.

## 2. Obligations

Each Party will:
1. Use the other Party's Confidential Information only for purposes
   related to the underlying Closer Agreement.
2. Protect it with the same care used for its own confidential
   information (no less than reasonable care).
3. Not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent,
   except to its own employees, contractors, or advisors with a
   need-to-know who are themselves bound by confidentiality.

## 3. Exceptions

This NDA does not apply to information that:
1. Is or becomes public other than through breach of this NDA.
2. Was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure.
3. Is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's
   Confidential Information.
4. Is rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality
   restriction.
5. Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order,
   provided the receiving Party gives prompt notice to allow the
   disclosing Party to seek a protective order.

## 4. Term

This NDA's obligations survive for **3 years** after the termination
of the Closer Agreement. Trade secrets remain protected indefinitely
while they qualify as such under applicable law.

## 5. Return / Destruction

On termination of the Closer Agreement, each Party will return or
destroy the other's Confidential Information at the disclosing
Party's option, and certify destruction in writing within 30 days.

## 6. No License

Nothing in this NDA grants either Party a license to the other's
Confidential Information beyond the limited use permitted in §2.

## 7. Remedies

The Parties acknowledge that breach of this NDA may cause irreparable
harm for which monetary damages are inadequate. The non-breaching
Party may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies.

## 8. Governing Law

This NDA is governed by the laws of **{{operator_jurisdiction}}**.
