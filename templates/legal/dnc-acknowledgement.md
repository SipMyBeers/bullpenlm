# DNC + TCPA Compliance Acknowledgement

> **Required reading before any closer makes a real cold call.**

By signing this Acknowledgement, I, **{{closer_legal_name}}**,
acknowledge and agree:

## 1. National DNC Registry

I understand that the National Do-Not-Call Registry (donotcall.gov)
contains millions of phone numbers that consumers have registered as
not wanting to receive telemarketing calls.

I will **not** dial any number on the National DNC Registry, except
in the following specific cases:
1. The called party has given me prior express written consent to be
   contacted at that number, **or**
2. The called party is an existing customer of {{operator_entity}}
   with an active business relationship (under FTC's established
   business relationship exemption, narrowly construed), **or**
3. The call is exempt under FTC TSR (e.g., calls to businesses, not
   covered by the residential DNC list).

## 2. State DNC Lists

I understand that some states maintain their own DNC lists in addition
to the federal registry, and that some states have stricter rules
(shorter business-relationship window, additional protected
categories).

I will use the BullpenLM platform's DNC scrub feature, which checks
both the National Registry and the relevant state list before
allowing me to claim a prospect.

## 3. Hours of Operation

Under the FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule, I will only place
telemarketing calls between **8:00 AM and 9:00 PM in the called
party's local time**. Some states are stricter:

- Alabama: 8 AM - 8 PM, no Sundays
- Arkansas: 8 AM - 9 PM, no Sundays
- Florida: 8 AM - 8 PM Mon-Sat, no Sundays or holidays
- Louisiana: 8 AM - 8 PM
- Mississippi: 8 AM - 8 PM

The BullpenLM platform's hours-of-day enforcement will block dials
outside permitted hours. I will not attempt to work around it.

## 4. Call Recording — Two-Party Consent

I understand that in two-party-consent states (CA, FL, IL, MD, MA,
MT, NV, NH, OR, PA, WA, CT, DE, HI), **both** parties must consent to
the recording of the call.

If either my state or the called party's state is a two-party-consent
state, I will **explicitly request and receive verbal consent** at
the start of the call, before discussing any substantive content.

Example script the platform will surface:
> "Hi, this is {{closer_first_name}} calling from {{operator_entity}}.
> This call may be recorded for quality and training purposes. Is
> that okay with you?"

If the called party says no, I will either continue the call without
recording (and note this in the system) or end the call politely.

## 5. Identification Requirement

Under the TSR, I will identify myself at the start of every call:
1. My name (or assumed business name registered with the FTC)
2. The company on whose behalf I'm calling ({{operator_entity}})
3. The purpose of the call (sales)
4. The nature of the goods or services being offered

## 6. Do-Not-Call Requests

If a prospect asks me, in any form, not to be called again, I will:
1. Honor the request immediately on the call.
2. Mark the prospect as opted-out in the BullpenLM platform within
   24 hours.
3. Add the number to {{operator_entity}}'s internal opt-out list,
   which the platform applies before every future scrub.

The opt-out applies to all future calls from {{operator_entity}}, not
just my own.

## 7. No Misrepresentation

I will not:
- Impersonate any other company or person
- Misrepresent the product, pricing, terms, refund policy, warranty,
  or any other material fact
- Claim false affiliations with government agencies, regulators, or
  consumer organizations

## 8. No Auto-Dialers / Pre-Recorded Messages

I will use the BullpenLM platform's manual-dial flow. I will not use
any external auto-dialer, predictive dialer, or pre-recorded message
(robocall) system to contact prospects, unless prior express written
consent is on file in the BullpenLM platform for that specific
prospect.

## 9. Penalties Are Real

I understand that TCPA violations can carry per-call statutory
penalties of $500-$1,500 (treble for knowing violations). Class
actions are common. These penalties can apply personally to me as
the calling party, not only to {{operator_entity}}.

Use the tools. Don't shortcut compliance. The platform makes this
trivial.

generally, exposes me to personal liability and indemnification
