## 1. Independent Contractor Relationship

1.1. Closer is engaged by Operator as an **independent contractor**, not
as an employee, agent, joint venturer, or partner of Operator.

1.2. Closer is **free from direction and control** by Operator over the
manner and means by which Closer performs services under this Agreement,
subject only to the deliverables, ethical standards, and compliance
obligations expressly set forth herein.

1.3. Closer is **engaged in an independently established trade,
occupation, or business** of the same nature as the services performed
hereunder.

1.4. Closer's services are **outside the usual course of Operator's
business** to the extent required by applicable law in
{{operator_jurisdiction}}.

1.5. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship,
joint venture, partnership, or franchise between the Parties. Closer
shall not represent to any third party that Closer is an employee,
agent, or representative of Operator beyond the scope of services
described herein.

## 2. Scope of Services

2.1. Closer will provide the following services to Operator:
**{{services_scope}}** (e.g., "outbound sales prospecting and deal
closing for Operator's product line").

2.2. Closer determines when, where, and how to perform these services,
subject to:
- Lawful conduct under applicable telemarketing, consumer-protection,
  and privacy laws (see §6);
- Ethical conduct consistent with the Code of Conduct attached as
  Exhibit A;
- Reasonable customer-facing accuracy regarding Operator's product.

2.3. Closer may engage subcontractors only with Operator's prior
written consent. Closer is responsible for any subcontractor's
compliance with this Agreement.

2.4. Closer is free to provide services to other operators, businesses,
and clients during the term of this Agreement, subject to §5
(Confidentiality) and §7 (Non-Solicitation, narrow).

## 3. Compensation

3.1. Closer is compensated solely on a **commission basis**:
**{{commission_pct}}%** of the net invoiced value of each deal closed
by Closer, where "closed" means: a counterparty has executed Operator's
agreement and Operator has received the first payment from that
counterparty.

3.2. Operator pays Closer's commission within **{{payment_days}} days**
of receipt of cleared funds from the counterparty, via
**{{payment_rail}}** (Stripe / Wise / Zelle / ACH / USDC) to Closer's
designated account.

3.3. **No salary, no hourly wage, no guaranteed minimum.** Closer's
earnings depend entirely on Closer's sales performance.

3.4. **No earnings for recruitment.** Closer earns no compensation,
bonus, or credit for introducing other closers to Operator's bullpen,
for referring other operators to the BullpenLM platform, or for any
other recruitment activity. All compensation is for deals closed with
real external customers.

3.5. **Expenses:** Closer bears Closer's own ordinary business expenses
(phone, internet, workspace, vehicle, etc.). Operator does not
reimburse expenses except by separate written agreement.

3.6. **Chargebacks:** If a closed deal refunds, charges back, or
otherwise fails within {{chargeback_window}} days, the corresponding
commission is reversed and Closer's account is debited accordingly.

## 4. Term and Termination

4.1. This Agreement begins on **{{signed_date}}** and continues until
terminated by either Party.

4.2. **Either Party may terminate** this Agreement at any time, with or
without cause, on {{notice_days}} days' written notice. "Written
notice" includes an email to the Party's notice address recorded in
this Agreement.

4.3. **Immediate termination for cause** is permitted by either Party
in the event of: material breach uncured for 14 days after written
notice; fraud; conduct that exposes the other Party to legal
liability; or insolvency.

4.4. On termination: (a) Closer ceases all sales activities on
Operator's behalf; (b) Closer's pending deals remain Closer's for
commission purposes if closed within {{posttermination_window}} days,
subject to §3.6; (c) confidentiality obligations under §5 survive.

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1. **Confidential Information** means non-public information of
Operator that Closer learns in connection with this Agreement,
including customer lists, pricing, product roadmap, sales scripts, and
internal communications.

5.2. Closer will not disclose Confidential Information to any third
party, and will not use it for any purpose other than performing
services under this Agreement, during the term and for 3 years
thereafter.

5.3. Closer's obligations under §5 do not apply to information that:
becomes public other than through Closer's act or omission; was known
to Closer before disclosure; is independently developed; or is
required to be disclosed by law (with prompt notice to Operator).

5.4. **Closer's own information** (Closer's general sales skill,
methodology, and post-engagement memory) remains Closer's own. This
section does not enjoin Closer from working in the same general field
after termination.

## 6. Compliance

6.1. **TCPA / DNC.** Closer will not call any number on the National
Do-Not-Call Registry, any applicable state DNC list, or Operator's
internal opt-out list. Closer will dial only during permitted hours
under the FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule and applicable state law.
Operator's BullpenLM platform provides DNC scrub tooling; Closer is
responsible for using it before each dial.

6.2. **Recording.** Where the called party's state or Closer's state
requires two-party consent for call recording, Closer will obtain
explicit verbal consent at the start of the call, before any
substantive sales content. Lack of consent ends the call.

6.3. **Truthful sales.** Closer will not misrepresent Operator's
product, pricing, terms, or affiliation. Closer will not impersonate
any other party.

6.4. **No anti-money-laundering violations, no bribery, no
unauthorized practice of any regulated profession** (legal, medical,
financial advice).

6.5. **Data handling.** Closer will treat all customer data per
Operator's privacy policy and applicable law (CCPA, GDPR, state
privacy laws). Closer will not exfiltrate customer data on
termination; on termination, Closer returns or destroys all customer
data in Closer's possession.

## 7. Non-Solicitation (Narrow)

7.1. For **{{nonsolicit_months}}** months after termination, Closer
will not solicit any Operator customer whose account Closer worked on
during the last 6 months of this Agreement, for the purpose of
selling a competing product.

7.2. This section is narrowly tailored to protect Operator's
customer relationships actually worked by Closer. It does not prevent
Closer from working in the same industry, calling on the broader
market, or accepting business from former Operator customers who
approach Closer first.

## 8. Indemnification

8.1. Closer indemnifies Operator for losses arising from Closer's
breach of §6 (Compliance), including TCPA violations, recording
violations, and misrepresentation claims.

8.2. Operator indemnifies Closer for losses arising from defects in
Operator's product itself (warranty claims, product liability) where
Closer's representations were consistent with materials Operator
provided.

## 9. BullpenLM Platform Acknowledgement

9.1. Closer acknowledges that **BullpenLM (a product of Beers Labs LLC)
is the software platform** used to organize sales work under this
Agreement.

9.2. Closer acknowledges that **BullpenLM is not a Party to this
Agreement**. BullpenLM is not Closer's employer. BullpenLM does not
pay Closer. BullpenLM does not hold any commission funds.

9.3. **Remedy for non-payment runs to Operator**, in
{{operator_jurisdiction}}. Closer waives any claim against BullpenLM /
Beers Labs LLC for compensation owed under this Agreement.

## 10. Dispute Resolution

10.1. The Parties will negotiate in good faith for at least 30 days
before initiating any formal proceeding.

10.2. **Governing law:** the laws of {{operator_jurisdiction}}, without
regard to conflict-of-law principles.

10.3. **Venue:** state or federal courts located in
{{operator_jurisdiction}}. The Parties consent to personal
jurisdiction there.

## 11. Miscellaneous

11.1. **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement (with Exhibit A) is the
entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes any prior
discussions.

11.2. **Amendment.** Amendments must be in writing and signed by both
Parties.

11.3. **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the
remainder of the Agreement remains in effect.

11.4. **No waiver.** Failure to enforce any provision is not a waiver
of the right to enforce it later.

11.5. **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed electronically
in counterparts; together they constitute one Agreement. Electronic
signature via the BullpenLM platform (typed name + hash of this
document) is sufficient.
