## 1. Your contracting party is {{operator_entity}}

**Not BullpenLM. Not Beers Labs LLC. Not the BullpenLM platform.**

## 2. BullpenLM is software

BullpenLM does not employ you. BullpenLM does not pay you. BullpenLM
does not direct your hours or your work. BullpenLM does not hold any of
your commission funds. We provide tooling: legal templates, training

Per the Closer Agreement you are about to sign, {{operator_entity}}
will pay you on the rail specified in that Agreement (Stripe / Wise /

against BullpenLM or Beers Labs LLC for compensation owed under your

## 4. You are an independent contractor for tax purposes

You will receive a **1099-NEC** at year-end from {{operator_entity}}
for any year in which {{operator_entity}} paid you $600 or more.

You are responsible for:
- Your own federal income tax
- Self-employment tax (15.3% — covers your share + the "employer"
  share since you have no employer)
- State and local taxes
- Quarterly estimated tax payments if you owe more than $1,000/year
- Any business expenses you incur (phone, internet, vehicle,
  workspace, etc.) — these may be deductible; consult a tax
  professional

You should plan to set aside roughly **25-35% of every commission
payment** for tax purposes. The exact amount depends on your total
income and your state.

## 5. Earnings depend on your performance

No specific income is promised. Past performance of other closers does
not predict yours. Sales work involves substantial uncertainty.
**You may earn zero.** You should be financially prepared for that
possibility.

## 6. No earnings come from recruiting other closers

If you introduce another closer to a bullpen, you earn **nothing** for
the referral. No commission, no bonus, no rank, no XP. This is a hard
platform rule — not a soft preference. BullpenLM's structure rewards
deals closed with real customers; it never rewards recruitment.

You also earn nothing for recruiting other operators to the BullpenLM
platform. The platform's leaderboards and rank systems are vanity
displays; they do not affect what you are paid.

## 7. Legal templates are starting points, not guarantees

BullpenLM does not warrant that the Closer Agreement template is
legally sufficient for your particular situation. The template is
generic; your situation may have specifics counsel should address.

**You may have an attorney review the Closer Agreement before signing.**
This is your right. {{operator_entity}} should respect a reasonable
request to delay signing while you consult counsel.

You may decline to sign the Closer Agreement and walk away from this
opportunity. There is no penalty for declining.

If anything in the Closer Agreement is unclear, ask

## 9. Compliance is your obligation too

When you sign the Closer Agreement, you take on obligations under
TCPA (Telephone Consumer Protection Act), the FTC Telemarketing
Sales Rule, recording-consent laws, and consumer privacy laws.
{{operator_entity}}'s tools (DNC scrub, hours-of-day checks,
two-party-consent prompts) help, but the obligations remain yours.

A TCPA violation can carry a per-call statutory penalty of $500-$1,500.
This is not theoretical; aggressive plaintiffs' attorneys monitor for
violations. Use the tools. Don't dial outside permitted hours. Don't
dial DNC-listed numbers.
