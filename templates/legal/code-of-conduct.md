# Exhibit A — Closer Code of Conduct

> Standard behavioral expectations for closers operating under a
> BullpenLM Closer Agreement. Attached to and incorporated into every
> Closer Agreement.

## 1. Honesty
- Identify yourself by your real name when asked.
- Identify the company you're representing ({{operator_entity}}) accurately.
- Do not misrepresent the product, pricing, terms, or your role.
- Do not impersonate any other person or company.

## 2. Compliance
- Use the BullpenLM DNC scrub before every dial.
- Dial only during permitted hours (8 AM - 9 PM called party local
  time, more restrictive in some states; the platform enforces this).
- Obtain explicit two-party recording consent in states that require it
  (the platform prompts you).
- Do not use any auto-dialer, predictive dialer, or pre-recorded
  message without a TCPA-compliant prior express written consent on
  file.
- Do not contact persons on Operator's internal opt-out list. If a
  prospect asks to be removed, mark them opted-out immediately.

## 3. Customer respect
- If a prospect says "no" or "not interested" clearly, end the call
  professionally. One follow-up attempt later (next quarter or longer)
  is acceptable; persistent re-dialing is not.
- Do not pressure, threaten, or harass.
- Do not exploit personal hardship (medical emergency, bereavement,
  financial distress) to close a deal.

## 4. Data handling
- Treat all prospect data as confidential.
- Do not share prospect data with anyone outside the bullpen.
- Do not export, copy, or retain prospect data after termination of
  the Closer Agreement.

## 5. Conflicts of interest
- Disclose to Operator any pre-existing relationship with a prospect
  that might affect the engagement.
- Do not double-bill: do not close the same deal under two different
  agreements.

## 6. Professional standards
- Show up to drills, meetings, and floor activities you committed to.
- Respect teammates. The bullpen is collaborative even though
  compensation is individual.
- Maintain reasonable English (or the locale's relevant language)
  command for the markets you dial.

## 7. Compensation rules (restated for clarity)
- Earnings come only from deals closed with real external customers.
- Earnings do not come from recruiting other closers or operators.
- Operator pays directly per the Closer Agreement; BullpenLM does not
  pay closers.

## 8. Escalations
- If you encounter behavior from Operator that contradicts this Code
  of Conduct, the BullpenLM project maintains a contact at the
  open-source repo (`https://github.com/SipMyBeers/bullpenlm`) for
  reporting. BullpenLM cannot adjudicate disputes — but persistent
  bad-actor patterns may result in an operator being removed from
  BullpenLM-hosted spaces (Discord, marketing).

## 9. Termination triggers
Material violation of this Code of Conduct is cause for immediate
termination of the Closer Agreement by Operator. Examples of material
violations include:
- TCPA violations
- Misrepresentation of product or affiliation
- Exfiltration of prospect data
- Harassment of prospects or teammates

---

By signing the Closer Agreement to which this Exhibit is attached,
Closer agrees to abide by this Code of Conduct.
